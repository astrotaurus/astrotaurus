GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions,
answered.

Everything you need to know before you order.

What information do I need? +

For a birth chart reading: your birth date, birth time, and birth city and country. For compatibility: those details for both people. You can also share what you'd most like the reading to focus on.

What if I don't know my birth time? +

You can still request a reading and select “I don't know.” Some placements and house interpretations depend on an exact time, so the reading will explain what can and cannot be interpreted reliably.

When will my reading arrive? +

Within 3–4 days after payment and all required birth details are received. Your reading will be sent to the email address you enter at checkout.

How will I receive it? +

Your written reading will arrive by email. Please check that your email address is correct when submitting your details.

Is this a prediction of my future? +

No. These readings are for personal reflection and entertainment. Astrology offers a way to explore patterns and possibilities, not guaranteed outcomes.
